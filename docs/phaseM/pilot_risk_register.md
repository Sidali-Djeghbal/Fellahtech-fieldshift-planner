# Phase M — Field Pilot Risk Register & Safety Mitigations

> **Scope:** Operational, scientific, human-factors, and software/database risks identified ahead of the Guelma Wilaya supervised field pilot (`PLANNED`).

| Risk ID | Risk Category | Description & Observed Project Basis | Severity | Mitigation Protocol Before / During Pilot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PM-01` | **False Yield / Profit Expectation** | Farmers may treat Phase I composite scores (`0.87`) as predicted grain yield (`t/ha`) or guaranteed profit despite absent local economic budgets (`income_potential = UNKNOWN`). | **CRITICAL** | Enumerators & advisors verbally state non-yield/non-profit scope; UI card banner + [`ExpertReviewRecord`](file:///home/lokman/Desktop/FeildShit-SI-Model/src/pilot/models.py#L99) `uncertainty_communication_clear` gate must confirm comprehension. |
| `PM-02` | **Direct Autonomous Farmer Acting Before Expert Review** | Sharing unvetted `CONDITIONAL_RECOMMENDATION_REQUIRES_LOCAL_VERIFICATION` output where local `DEM` puddling or `Orobanche` seedbank invalidates a crop sequence. | **CRITICAL** | Human-in-the-loop gate: recommendations stay advisor-only (`approved_for_farmer_briefing=false`) until `ITGC`/`DSA`/`ONID` expert signs off (`ExpertReviewRecord`). |
| `PM-03` | **Seybouse / Well Salinity Damage (`ECe` / `ECw`)** | Irrigated recommendations (`TOMATO_IND`, pulses) carry `RULE_SALINITY_UNMEASURED_IRRIGATION = UNKNOWN` because spatial salinity data do not exist locally. | **HIGH** | Target irrigated Seybouse/Bouchekouf/well parcels for Section C portable `ECw`/`ECe` measurements; flag `REQUIRES_ADDITIONAL_INFO` if farmer reports salt crusts without lab test. |
| `PM-04` | **Heavy Clay Winter Waterlogging (`DEM` Absent)** | `Belkheir`, `Boumahra`, `Bouchekouf` alluvial clay loams (`~33–36%` clay) suffer ponding in wet winters (`2019` `835.6 mm`), while DEM is `UNAVAILABLE_NOT_INTEGRATED`. | **HIGH** | Use Phase F `Q_DRAINAGE_QUALITATIVE` field prompt + visual advisor drainage check during parcel intake; log missing relief via `MISSING_DEM_DRAINAGE_CONTEXT`. |
| `PM-05` | **Language Barrier (`en` Prose vs. `ar`/`fr` Farmers)** | Phase L audit verified API/Phase J explanation strings remain English even if `language="fr"` or `"ar"` is set. | **HIGH** | Pair trained bilingual (`Arabic`/`French`/`English`) `DSA`/`ITGC` advisors/enumerators with every pilot session; prioritize FR/AR rule templates post-pilot. |
| `PM-06` | **`SoilGrids 250 m` Sub-Pixel / Urban Masking (`< 5 ha`)** | Observed in Phase K (`1.79 ha` Belkheir `clay=null`; `Oued Zenati` `clay=null`, `soc=null`): 250 m pixels mask edge plots or smooth small anomalies. | **MEDIUM-HIGH** | Sampling plan deliberately includes `>= 11` `< 5 ha` plots; compare Section B expert/Section C lab soil texture (`RMSE`/bias) rather than assuming 250 m fidelity. |
| `PM-07` | **Drought Boundary Fragility (`GPM` vs `NASA POWER`)** | Observed `PRECIP_SENSITIVE_RANK_SHIFT` in northern `2022` drought (`444.04 mm` GPM vs `608.94 mm` POWER around `FABA_BEAN` `450 mm` floor). | **MEDIUM-HIGH** | Advisors explicitly present rainfed drought contingency (`Barley/Chickpea` backup) whenever `PRECIP_SENSITIVE_RANK_SHIFT` appears. |
| `PM-08` | **Unverified Local Crops (`POTATO`, `SUNFLOWER`)** | Both carry `HYPOTHESIS_NEEDS_DSA_CHECK` in `crop_profiles.json` and lack full Guelma field calibration. | **MEDIUM** | Do not brief farmers to plant Sunflower or Potato autonomously; use Section B review specifically to collect DSA verdict on these two systems. |
| `PM-09` | **Unconnected Auth & Single-File SQLite Pilot Storage** | `PostgreSQL+PostGIS` & multi-tenant OAuth2 auth remain `DISCONNECTED`; pilot server runs locally on `SQLite` without host-level multi-user RBAC. | **MEDIUM** | Run supervised pilot on password-protected advisor laptops / local secure LAN backed up daily (`sqlite` snapshots + JSON exports); never expose unauthenticated localhost publicly on internet. |
| `PM-10` | **Field Cadastre Polygon Drawing Error on Mobile** | Farmers drawing freehand vertices on small/irregular parcels could drop a polygon onto an adjacent orchard/road or trigger out-of-Wilaya bounds. | **LOW-MEDIUM** | Enumerator walks/inspects parcel landmarks on map canvas, confirms `area_ha` and commune via `POST /api/v1/fields/validate` before submitting `/analyze`. |
