## 3. Operational Spatial-Resolution Safety Verdict

- **Prohibited Actions:** Do **NOT** extrapolate 10–250 m SoilGrids/MODIS/Sentinel-2 products into synthetic `N/P/K`, `ECe`, `FC/PWP`, or grain yield numbers; do **NOT** downscale seasonal `11 km` GPM (`-132.3 mm/yr` bias) into monthly/parcel water balances without explicit dual-source bias sensitivity; do **NOT** apply `55–69 km` 10-yr thermal means to daily frost/heat kill thresholds.
- **Permitted Safe Actions:** Keep `crop_frequency_2021_2025` and `s2_2024_03_ndvi_10m` strictly as parcel cropland eligibility checks; retain dual GPM + NASA POWER annual precipitation sensitivity ranges with explicit bias disclosure; prioritize `DEM` (30 m), `WS2M` (POWER AG), and explicit API capture fields (`soil_ece_dsm`, `water_ecw_dsm`, `water_sar`, `fc`, `pwp`, `pest_history`) prior to hydrological or agronomic score redesign.
