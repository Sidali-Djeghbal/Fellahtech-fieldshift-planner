# Candidate Modeling Approach Evaluation — Field Shift (Phase E)

**Evaluation Criteria:** Local data availability (Phases A–D), agronomic explainability, structural handling of multi-year crop sequence rules, robustness to missing targets/coarse scales, and validation tractability.

## 2. Explicit Justification for Rejecting Supervised ML in Phase E/F

1. **Missing Target (`Y`):** Supervised suitability, rotation, or yield modeling requires ground-truth observations ($Y_{\text{crop}}$, $Y_{\text{yield}}$, or $Y_{\text{rotation outcome}}$). No such labels exist anywhere in `data/` ([Guelma_Annual_Seasonal_Crop_Analysis.csv](file:///home/lokman/Desktop/FeildShit-SI-Model/data/tables/Guelma_Annual_Seasonal_Crop_Analysis.csv) is merely an `NDVI > 0.4` regional greenness sum).
2. **Prohibition Against Synthetic Labels:** Generating synthetic crop-suitability scores via expert rules and then training XGBoost to mimic those rules adds black-box opacity with zero empirical validity ("circular modeling").
3. **Future Readiness:** The proposed **Hybrid Architecture** cleanly exposes an optional **Phase G Empirical Calibration Hook** so once DSA municipal campaign yields, parcel crop surveys, or GEE `10 m` Sentinel-2 phenology labels are collected, calibrated empirical response curves ($K_y$ yield ratios or spatial crop priors) can plug directly into Stage 4–5 without rewriting the pipeline.
